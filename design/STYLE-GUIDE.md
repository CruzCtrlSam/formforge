# FormForge visual guide

Use approved-concept.png as the reference for composition, athletic energy, photography, and badge styling.

## Colors

- Black #111510 for page backgrounds.
- Lime #D6F783 for primary actions, progress, and the logo.
- Hot rose #FF5C9D for selected options, focus indicators, badge details, and small highlights.
- Cream #F5F2E9 for primary text.
- Panel #1B211C and border #39443A for surfaces.

Use dark text on solid lime or pink. Keep pink accents sparse.

## Typography and composition

Use bold condensed uppercase display headlines and readable sentence-case body text. Current display fallback is Impact / Arial Narrow. Use large touch controls and quiet spacing around instructions.

## Photography

Use realistic adults in ordinary workout clothing, home settings, strong side lighting, charcoal and olive surroundings, and natural skin tones. Keep instructional images clear and evenly framed. Generate start and finish frames together with matching subject, clothing, camera, and environment. Review anatomy and exercise form before use.

## Badges

Use a shared shield outline, lime details, dark interior, and bold cream labels. Pink can mark a small achievement detail. Reuse the master shape and label treatment for each badge.

## Review

Compare new assets with the approved concept at phone size. Check palette, typography, crop, readable labels, and exercise positioning. The concept includes sample content and is a visual reference.
